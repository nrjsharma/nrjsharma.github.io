Road Rage Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Road Rage Rush is a traffic-light game for Android made by nrjsharma (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

In short

Road Rage Rush does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account to create, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game saves, and where

To remember how far you have got, the game saves these on your device:

  • the stars you have earned on each map and difficulty;
  • your Sound and Music volume settings.

This information stays on your device. It is never sent to me or to anyone else, and I cannot see it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android’s settings, deletes it.

Internet and permissions

The game plays entirely on your device and does not send any data over the internet. It does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Device backups

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the game’s saved progress in your device backup with Google, so it can be restored on a new phone or tablet. That backup is run by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy, and I have no access to it.

Google Play

You download the game from Google Play, which Google runs under its own Privacy Policy. Google gives developers summary statistics, such as the number of installs, and crash reports from people who have chosen to share diagnostics with Google. These do not tell me who you are, and I use them only to fix problems and improve the game.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes any of this, for example by adding advertising or online features, I will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your privacy, email nrjsharma.dev@gmail.com.
